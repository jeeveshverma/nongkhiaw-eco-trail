    Tour code 001

    Amazing 1 Day Boat Trip to Muang Ngoi

    Culture, trekking, waterfall and kayaking in one unforgettable day. Boat upriver, walk through a village, swim at Tad Mok, then paddle home.

    USD 30per person
    Book this tour

    The day, step by step

    1. 8:30 am

      Slow boat up the Nam Ou

      A scenic boat ride upstream along the Nam Ou river to Ban Muang Ngoi.

    2. Morning

      Walk through Muang Ngoi

      Your guide leads you through the village, with a real look at local culture and daily life.

    3. Late morning

      Historic cave and viewpoint

      Hike to a historic cave, then continue up to a breathtaking viewpoint over the valley.

    4. Midday

      Rice paddies to Tad Mok

      A short boat ride to Ban SopKong, then a peaceful trek through seasonal rice paddies to the beautiful Tad Mok waterfall. Swim in the natural pool.

    5. Lunch

      Picnic lunch

      A delicious picnic lunch after your swim. Vegetarian options available.

    6. Afternoon

      Organic farm stop

      Enjoy coffee or a smoothie at an organic farm (drinks not included in the tour).

    7. Late afternoon

      Kayak back to Nong Khiaw

      Paddle home surrounded by mountains and gentle river views.

    Why “Eco-Trail”

    Sharing our home, our culture and our nature.

    Nong Khiaw Eco-Trail is run by a local team from Nong Khiaw and the surrounding villages. We have been organizing local tours since 2024, sharing our home, culture and nature with travelers.

    We call it “Eco-Trail” because we focus on responsible travel, supporting local communities and protecting the natural environment.

    Quick answers

    How much is the 1-day tour?

    USD 30 per person.

    What time does it start?

    The day starts at 8:30 am with a scenic boat ride upstream on the Nam Ou.

    What is included?

    An English-speaking guide, entrance fee tickets to sightseeing, 2 bottles of drinking water and lunch.

    Yes, vegetarian options are available for the picnic lunch.

    What should I bring?

    Mosquito repellent, comfortable shoes, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, toiletries, a swimming suit and a change of clothes.
